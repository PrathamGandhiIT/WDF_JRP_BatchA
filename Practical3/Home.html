About StudentHub

StudentHub is a single portal for students, faculty and admins to manage college life. From checking attendance to uploading study material and joining campus events, everything is available in one dashboard so nobody has to run between different offices or websites.

Who is StudentHub For?

🎓

Students

Track attendance, view marks, download resources, and stay on top of assignments.

📚

Faculty

Upload study material, manage class lists, and post announcements to students.

🛠️

Admins

Oversee students and faculty, manage courses, and view college-wide reports.
